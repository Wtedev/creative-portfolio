# Reference Image Requirements

## Required Before Phase 3

**The four previously approved reference images must be attached to Cursor at the beginning of Phase 3.**

Do not begin final visual design without them.

## What to Study

- Purple volumetric lighting
- Dark technical backgrounds
- Fine grid systems
- Glass-like cards
- Large editorial typography
- Cyan and magenta accent lighting
- Interactive technology mood

## What Not to Do

References inform direction — they must **not** be copied literally.

The final interface must avoid:

- Pure black (`#000`) or pure white (`#fff`)
- Excessive glow
- Poor text contrast
- Overuse of 3D
- Constant visual noise
- Dense card layouts
- Effects that reduce performance

## Approved Color Foundation (Starting Point)

```text
Dark background:  #111017
Dark surface:     #191721
Electric violet:  #9A6CFF
Soft violet:      #C2A7FF
Cyan accent:      #58D6F7
Light background: #F3F1F5
```

These tokens exist in `src/styles/tokens.css` but final sizing, spacing, and effects are Phase 3 deliverables.
