# Product Brief

## Purpose

This portfolio serves two business goals:

1. Help companies, agencies, studios, and creative teams evaluate the owner for a **full-time role**.
2. Help companies and brands hire her for **selected freelance projects**.

## Positioning

- Art Direction
- Visual identities
- Campaign direction
- Visual systems
- Creative websites
- Digital experiences
- Creative development
- Motion and interaction
- Technology used as a creative tool

## Experience Qualities

Strategic, creative, technically advanced, luminous, modern, editorial, direct, easy to understand, smooth and responsive. **Work remains more important than visual effects.**
